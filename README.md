### 👋 I build AI companies, not just AI demos

I run **[AI Venture Holdings](https://aiventureholdings.com)** — a Utah-based AI venture studio. We take an idea through **01 Discover → 02 Build → 03 Launch → 04 Scale**, and every venture ships as a real product on a real domain. Everything below is live or in active build, and most of it is open for contributors.

- **Everyone:** ⭐ the repos you find useful — it's how others find them too
